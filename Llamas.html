    Various types of Llamas

    • Suri Llama
      • Recognized by their distinctive non-crimped fleece that hangs down in long, silky, twisting dreadlocks.
    • Pack Llama
      • Pack llamas are draft animals trained to carry cargo across rough terrain, favored for their low environmental impact, surefootedness, and gentle demeanor.
    • Show Llama
      • Bred and groomed to meet strict aesthetic standards, conformation, and temperament for competition in livestock shows.
    • Guard Llama
      • Naturally protective animals kept to watch over sheep, goats, or livestock and ward off predators like coyotes or stray dogs.
    • Huacaya Llama
      • Characterized by dense, crimped, fleece that grows perpendicular to the skin, giving them a distinctly fluffy, teddy-bear appearance.
    • Fibre Llama
      • Bred specifically for high-quality fleece production, generating a soft, warm, and durable wool used for textiles.
